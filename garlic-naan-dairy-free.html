Garlic Naa (Dairy Free)

Soft and fluffy flatbread infused with fresh garlic and herbs.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp instant yeast
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • ¾ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • ¾ cup warm water
  • 1½ tbsp olive oil or avocado oil
  • 1 clove garlic, minced, optional
  • 1 tbsp chopped cilantro, optional

Instructions

  1. Make the dough: In a large bowl, combine the flour, yeast, sugar, salt, and baking powder. Add the warm water and oil, then mix until a dough forms.
  2. Knead the dough: Knead for 5–7 minutes, until the dough is smooth, soft, and slightly tacky. If it is too sticky, add flour 1 tbsp at a time.
  3. Let the dough rise: Place the dough in a lightly oiled bowl, cover, and let it rise in a warm area for 60–90 minutes, or until roughly doubled in size.
  4. Divide the dough: Punch the dough down and divide it into 6 equal pieces. Roll each piece into a ball, cover, and let rest for 10 minutes.
  5. Shape the naan: Roll each ball into a thin oval, approximately ⅛-inch thick. If making garlic naan, press minced garlic and chopped cilantro into the surface of the dough.
  6. Heat the pan: Heat a cast-iron skillet or heavy stainless-steel pan over medium-high to high heat until very hot. Do not add oil.
  7. Cook the naan: Place the naan in the hot pan and cook for 60–90 seconds, until bubbles form and the bottom develops dark brown spots. Flip and cook for another 30–60 seconds, until lightly charred.
  8. Finish the naan: Remove from the pan and immediately brush with a little olive oil. Finish with fresh cilantro, a pinch of salt, and additional minced garlic if desired. Serve warm.
